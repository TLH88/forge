# Privacy policy for The Forge

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The Forge is a free, non-commercial skin and helper add-on for Kodi, made by Merlin88 (Foundry 88 Labs). This page says what it does with your information.

## The short version

The Forge has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing about you or what you watch is sent to its developer. Everything it stores stays on the device Kodi runs on.

## What is stored on your device

- **Sign-in tokens.** When you sign in to a service through the Forge Helper (Trakt, and optionally a debrid service or metadata service), the access token that service issues is saved in the helper's data folder in your Kodi profile. The helper copies it into the settings of the add-ons that need it on the same device, so you only sign in once. Your passwords are never seen or stored: sign-in happens on the service's own website with a one-time code.
- **Your choices.** Setup progress and options you pick in the helper.
- **Caches.** Movie and set details fetched from TMDb, and artwork Kodi has shown, kept to make screens load faster. The helper removes cached artwork that has not been shown for a week.

Removing the add-ons, or clearing their data in Kodi, deletes all of it.

## Trakt

Signing in to Trakt is optional. If you do, The Forge and the add-ons it sets up use your Trakt account to:

- read your watched history, lists, watchlist and progress, to show them on screen;
- record what you watch on this device, and changes you make such as marking something watched.

That information travels directly between your device and Trakt. It is not sent anywhere else by The Forge. You can disconnect at any time from the helper's Accounts screen, or by revoking the app in your Trakt account settings.

The Forge uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

## Services your device talks to

Your device contacts these directly, and each has its own privacy policy:

- **Trakt** (trakt.tv), if you sign in.
- **TMDb** (themoviedb.org), for movie, show and set details and artwork.
- **GitHub** (github.com), to check for and download updates to The Forge.
- **Other services you choose to sign in to** through the helper.

Third-party Kodi add-ons that The Forge installs or works with are separate software with their own behavior.

## Children

The Forge is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
